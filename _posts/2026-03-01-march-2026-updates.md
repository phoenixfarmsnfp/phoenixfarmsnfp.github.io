## If you're new here...

Welcome! We are Phoenix Farms, a small nonprofit organization that maintains a food forest in Woodlawn. We've been operating for a few years now on God's Little Acre, a lot on 64th and Kimbark, in partnership with [First Presbyterian Church](https://www.chicagofirstchurch.org/).

Phoenix Farms seeks to promote access to fresh, sustainably-grown fruits and vegetables for our local community. Our food forest is open to the public, and we encourage everyone to pick and eat our produce completely free and at any time. Produce not picked by our community is donated to First Presbyterian's Free Food Market. More about the folks running the organization can be found on [our about page](https://www.phoenixfarmschicago.org/about).

## Food Forest Updates

The weather has been very Chicago - warm and sunny one day, then cold and snowing the next. Despite the variability, our trees are budding! We were so excited to see this sign of life and health from our little trees.

{% include blog-image.html image="3.2026/1.jpg" alt="Budding fruit tree at Phoenix Farms with First Presbyterian Church in the background" %}

Last year was the first year our trees flowered. It was thrilling, but we cut off the buds so the trees could focus on growing bigger and taller. This year, we're hopeful they'll produce fruit for us and our neighbors to enjoy at last. The first apples and cherries are going to be amazing.

We are hosting a spring workday in the coming weeks to gather as a community and prepare our little plot for a bountiful summer. Please join if you're able - no gardening or farming skills required, and we'll provide equipment. Details below.
