## If you're new here...

Welcome! We are Phoenix Farms, a small nonprofit organization that maintains a food forest in Woodlawn. We've been operating for a few years now on God's Little Acre, a lot on 64th and Kimbark, in partnership with [First Presbyterian Church](https://www.chicagofirstchurch.org/).

Phoenix Farms seeks to promote access to fresh, sustainably-grown fruits and vegetables for our local community. Our food forest is open to the public, and we encourage everyone to pick and eat our produce completely free and at any time. Produce not picked by our community is donated to First Presbyterian's Free Food Market. More about the folks running the organization can be found on [our about page](https://www.phoenixfarmschicago.org/about).

## Food Forest Updates

Besides our big social media update, we've been starting to think about next year's planting and forest improvements.

This summer, we'd like to plant a record number of native pollinator plants for us. We've noticed over the years the increase in butterfly and bee traffic that our native pollinators bring. These little pollinators not only help our fruit trees flourish, they make our whole neighborhood ecosystem stronger - their happiness is our happiness! In the next season we'll expand our growing space a bit to make room for more pollinator plants.

While these pollinator plants may not produce food for us, they indirectly boost our edible plants' production by feeding the pollinators that allow our tomatoes, fruit trees, and more to grow. That's the beauty of a balanced ecosystem - each piece benefits the next. If you're curious, the Chicago Botanic Garden has a great article on the benefits of pollinators. [Check it out here](https://www.chicagobotanic.org/plant-information/smart-gardener/plant-a-pollinator-strip)!
